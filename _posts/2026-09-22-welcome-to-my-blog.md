Welcome to my new digital space. As a student exploring backend software development using tools like Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL, I wanted a simple, lightweight place to share my projects and thoughts without the overhead of a heavy CMS.

### Why Static?

Building this with pure HTML/CSS and Jekyll means:
1. **Speed:** No database queries slowing things down.
2. **Security:** No backend vulnerabilities.
3. **Simplicity:** I can write my posts in Markdown and push to GitHub to deploy.

Stay tuned for more deep dives into C++, load balancing, and API development!
